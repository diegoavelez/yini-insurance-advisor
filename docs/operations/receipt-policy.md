# Yini Local Receipt Policy

## Universal Owner

The installed AgentOps Engineering resource `references/receipt-policy.md` is
the universal semantic owner for receipt classes, Capsule versus Full routing,
retention/access requirements, and sensitive evidence. This local projection
does not reproduce or weaken that contract.

## Provider-Eval Routing

Yini uses repository profile `provider-eval`.

- Ordinary local documentation or deterministic test delivery requires Receipt
  Capsule v1.
- `forensic-failure`, `security-data`, `provider-evaluation`,
  `external-publication`, and `release-production` route to the universal
  `FULL_REQUIRED` handling: Capsule plus sanitized Full receipt.
- A missing, unknown, ambiguous, or conflicting profile or class is `BLOCKED`
  for owner disposition.

These routes classify terminal evidence only. They grant no collection,
inspection, retention, deletion, review, Git, publication, provider, or
successor action.

## Local Projection

Every local terminal capsule identifies the task and fixed point, changed and
staged deltas, validation actually observed, evidence ceiling, residual risk,
and next owner gate. Commit, ref, provider, or external facts appear only when
actually observed under their own authority.

A required Full receipt points to its approved retention/access policy. If no
approved pointer exists, record the literal `UNAVAILABLE` and stop when the
universal policy requires a safe retention decision. A pointer is not access or
retention authority.

## Sensitive Evidence

Receipts and their index exclude credentials, tokens, secrets, personal data,
confidential source content, raw provider/client payloads, and unnecessary
logs. Prefer stable identifiers, paths, hashes, counts, bounded redactions, and
sanitized summaries. Do not fetch protected content merely to complete a
receipt.

## Prospective Indexing

`docs/operations/receipts/index.md` lists only sanitized durable receipts that
are separately authorized for repository persistence. Indexing does not make a
receipt current authority or live state.

## No Historical Backfill

The local index begins prospectively with no historical entries. Do not
reconstruct, infer, or fabricate past receipts or evidence. Historical specs,
commits, reports, and chat records do not become receipts retroactively.
