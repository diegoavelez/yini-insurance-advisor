## Shared grant and return contract

Each future grant names owner, work unit, executor, physical principal/common
dir, exact HEAD/ref/index and complete candidate/foreign identity, trusted
runtime/tools, scope, measurable predicate, validator argv, excluded actions,
finite counters and stops. Proposed ceilings are 2 eligible transient
identical retries before mutation, 1 invocation-only repair and 2 semantic
corrections after first complete validation. No balance transfer, observer
retry, terminal restart, hidden assertion change or automatic successor.

Each return includes actual deltas, checks, skipped work, every failure and
counter used, native evidence pointer, evidence ceiling and next owner gate.
Independent review and owner acceptance are separate even if the owner groups
their authorizations administratively. Ordinary local documentary/test work
uses Receipt Capsule v1; no Full receipt is manufactured for each tool call.
