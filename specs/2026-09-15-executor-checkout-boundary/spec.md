## Problem and Outcome

Free-form preflights have left room for executor redelegation, an overridden
initial cwd, confusion between an index file digest and a Git tree identity,
and searches against nonexistent source directories. Prior wrapper failures
also show that a successful tool envelope is insufficient evidence of complete
capture. Historical observations and their limits remain in
[validation.md](validation.md); this spec does not diagnose new incidents.

The intended outcome is one reusable, literal read-only recipe and one explicit
transition graph, both owned by [plan.md](plan.md). A future task can bind
known inputs, preserve each exit/result, distinguish recoverable capture from
real failure, and return a bounded receipt without inventing commands or
acquiring successor authority. This is contractual prevention, with no promise
of zero failures, measured efficiency, or per-task tool enforcement.

## Interfaces and Seams

| Seam | Input | Output and invariant |
|---|---|---|
| Owner to master | Exact action/task/fixed-point/scope grant | Administrative dispatch, never a broader grant |
| Received task to preflight | Role, dispatch_owner, checkout, manifest, commands, limits | Observed physical identity and complete fingerprint, or STOP |
| Read-only tool to evidence | Native envelope/output, exit, source and range | Complete bounded capture or explicit incomplete/error classification |
| Delivery to review | Exact candidate, canonical updates, checks, receipt | Fresh FULL or qualified NARROW_DELTA assessment under separate authority |
| Review to owner | Findings/PASS and exact evidence ceiling | Owner decision; no automatic action |

The C1 capture helper stays in the executor workflow. The recipe points to it
without copying its parser or treating it as an official tool schema. SHA-256
of raw `.git/index` bytes is not a tree OID; all manifests bind physical paths,
status, file kind/mode, and content identity without self-referential hashes.

## Risks, Definition of Done, and Next Decision

Main risks are confusing this proposal with live policy, broadening recovery
into mutation retry, stale fixed points, and overclaiming historical review or
runtime evidence. Explicit status, one recipe/graph owner, exact manifests,
separate authority checks, and the prospective matrix address those risks.

S1 is done when all five documents exist, historical meaning is retained,
S1-AC01 through S1-AC16 are traceable, authorized documentary checks pass,
nine protected paths and Git/marker identities are preserved, and the terminal
capsule reports all fourteen delta hashes/modes and unexecuted evidence.
